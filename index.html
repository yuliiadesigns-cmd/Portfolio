[01] About me

I design intuitive, user-centered interfaces that solve real problems. My process is rooted in deep research and hypothesis generation, followed by ideation, prototyping, and collaboration closely with interdisciplinary teams.

" In 2017, I built my first fintech product — a trading terminal bot — at a time when trading interfaces weren't yet mainstream. It was a challenge to define user patterns, quickly grasp complex mechanics, and design under tight deadlines. Later, at Infinity Reality, I faced a new kind of challenge: creating an immersive web experience alongside a user-friendly interface for a cutting-edge platform. Both experiences pushed me to balance innovation with usability and shaped how I approach complex design problems."
