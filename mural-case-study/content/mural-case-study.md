# Mural — Experts on Demand

## Case Study Goal
Create a senior product design case study about Mural’s B2B “Experts on Demand” initiative.

The case should not feel like a simple landing page redesign. It should feel like a strategic enterprise UX case about transforming a sales-led, high-touch service into a more scalable discovery and onboarding experience.

---

## Project Summary
I worked on the B2B direction of Mural, helping shape the Experts on Demand experience — a service layer that allowed enterprise teams to access Mural experts for workshop facilitation, custom templates, workflow setup, and collaboration consulting.

The initiative helped enterprise organizations use Mural not only as a shared workspace, but as a flexible collaboration environment supported by expert-led services.

---

## My Role
Product Designer

Responsibilities:
- Designed the landing / discovery experience
- Interviewed potential enterprise clients
- Interviewed Mural’s sales team to understand the existing B2B sales and onboarding process
- Analyzed how enterprise customers accessed services before
- Helped restructure how expert services were presented
- Helped bridge self-serve collaboration with expert-assisted workflows

---

## Context
Before this initiative, access to Mural’s enterprise support was mostly sales-led.

Enterprise customers typically moved through:
- sales conversations
- closed demos
- manual qualification
- high-touch onboarding
- custom discussions with the sales team

This process was personalized, but difficult to scale and hard for users to understand independently.

---

## Users
Primary users:
- Enterprise clients
- Large teams
- Corporate innovation teams
- Workshop organizers
- Team leads
- Internal facilitators
- Companies using Mural as a shared collaboration environment

These users needed one shared tool where teams could collaborate, use existing templates, and request expert support when their workflow required customization.

---

## Problem
Enterprise users needed more flexibility than a basic self-serve experience could provide.

They could log into Mural through their company domain and access existing templates and workspaces, but it was not always clear:
- what expert services were available
- how to request customized support
- when to use existing templates vs expert help
- how Mural experts could support workshops or team workflows
- how the B2B offering differed from the regular user experience

---

## Key UX Challenge
How might we transform a high-touch, sales-led enterprise support process into a clearer, more scalable discovery experience that helps teams choose between self-serve templates and expert-assisted collaboration support?

---

## Before
The previous B2B experience was mostly sales-guided.

Flow:
Enterprise interest
→ sales contact
→ closed demo
→ manual explanation of services
→ qualification
→ onboarding conversation
→ custom setup

This worked for personalized sales, but it made the service hard to discover and difficult to scale.

---

## After
The new experience introduced a clearer enterprise entry point.

Flow:
Enterprise user lands on Experts on Demand
→ understands available collaboration support
→ logs in with company domain
→ accesses existing workspace/templates
→ chooses between:
1. continue with existing templates/workflows
2. request tailored expert support
→ expert-assisted facilitation / template customization / workflow setup

---

## Core Product Model
The experience created a hybrid model:

Self-serve collaboration
+
Expert-assisted customization

Enterprise users could continue using existing templates, but they also had a clear path to request tailored support from Mural experts.

---

## Services Presented
Experts on Demand included:
- Workshop facilitation
- Collaboration consulting
- Team training
- Custom template design
- Workflow setup
- Team enablement

---

## Research Inputs
Research came from:
- interviews with potential enterprise clients
- conversations with Mural’s sales team
- analysis of the existing sales-led B2B process
- understanding where users needed more clarity before speaking to sales

Key insights:
- Enterprise users needed to understand the value before entering a sales conversation
- The offering had to communicate flexibility, not fixed packages only
- Users needed to see the difference between existing templates and custom support
- Sales knowledge needed to be translated into a scalable product experience

---

## Design Strategy
The strategy was to:
- clarify the value of Experts on Demand
- reduce dependency on closed demos for basic understanding
- make enterprise support options easier to discover
- show that expert help could be customized
- create a clear decision point between self-serve and expert-assisted paths
- support scalable onboarding for large teams

## Outcome
The work improved **scalable onboarding**, **clearer service discovery**, and **enterprise collaboration enablement** by giving teams an explicit path from self-serve templates to expert-assisted facilitation.

Operational flexibility improved: fewer teams needed a closed demo to understand value, while complex programs could still escalate into tailored expert engagements.

---

## Reflection
This project reinforced how enterprise products win when they **translate tacit sales knowledge into structured product surfaces**—without flattening the nuance that large buyers expect.

The core tension remains: **flexibility vs clarity**. A hybrid model works when the interface makes the decision moment legible, and when experts are positioned as accelerators—not gatekeepers.
